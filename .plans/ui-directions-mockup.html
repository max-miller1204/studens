Lesson + question

Moves and borrows

Rust drops a value when its owner goes out of scope, and it must drop it exactly once. If two bindings owned the same heap allocation, both would try to free it, and you would have a double free. Moving the value keeps the count at one.

both would try to free it…

Why a double free and not a leak?

Because drop is automatic. Each owner that leaves scope frees what it owns, so two owners free twice.
