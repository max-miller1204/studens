# Screenshots

[Documentation](README.md)

Classrooms collect lessons, reference sheets, and learning records. Group related
classrooms into folders without moving their saved files.

![Classrooms](../screenshots/classrooms.png)
![Lessons, scores, and question counts](../screenshots/classroom.png)

Highlight a passage to ask about it. Answers and follow-ups stay in an anchored
thread and survive reloads.

![Highlight to ask](../screenshots/highlight.png)
![A question with its passage](../screenshots/ask.png)
![An answer and follow-up](../screenshots/answer.png)

Quizzes support nine response types, partial credit, and saved feedback. New
lessons use pretests; due ideas return in spaced reviews. After a missed answer,
reply to the teacher's new question below the quiz.

![Per-question feedback](../screenshots/quiz.png)

Lessons support glossary recall, bundled diagrams and math, and light or dark themes.

![A lesson in dark mode](../screenshots/lesson-dark.png)

Screenshots use a disposable demo classroom. See [usage](USAGE.md) to get started
or the [teaching method](TEACHING.md) to author lessons.
